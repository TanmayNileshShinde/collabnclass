## 🎓 Classroom Privacy & Safety

- **Session Ephemerality:** Audio/video streams are transferred directly between peer participants in real-time. Classroom video streams are not recorded or persisted without explicit host activation.
- **Student Data Minimization:** We do not collect student browsing histories, device identifiers, or biometric profiles.
- **Zero Third-Party Marketing:** No pupil or educator data is sold or provided to advertising networks.
